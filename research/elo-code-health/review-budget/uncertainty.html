K is an update size; uncertainty needs its own model

These synthetic Beta distributions all predict a 50% chance of a finding. Their widths differ because their assumed prior evidence differs. They are illustrations, not calibrated posteriors for your code.

R = 1500 + 400 log₁₀(a/b). After a finding, a increases by 1; after a complete review with no finding, b increases by 1. The equivalent K generally differs by outcome. Unknown or unperformed reviews contribute neither outcome. Repeated reviews on one change are correlated and do not supply this model’s independent evidence.

Practical implication: record which snapshot and contracts each review covered, what its fixes changed, and whether a later finding was verified. Changing K cannot recover missing observations or distinguish reviewer sensitivity from code risk.
