# Cross-check of agentic practice research

Reviewed 2026-11-10 on nixed. Read [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) and sections 01–08. External checks used web search and primary papers, author pages, product documentation and public skill source. Local checks read the two `sessions.jsonl` files, aggregates and extraction code. No analysis pipeline, build or test suite was rerun. No commits or outreach were made.

## Top findings

- **B/C locally, A externally:** Make verification claims checkable before adding orchestration. Claude's later-round entries concentrate in persistence and contracts; Codex's scored replies repeatedly mention test quality. Mutation-guided testing and valid reproduction tests support checking fault detection and expected behavior. Neither dataset measures how much your full checklist improves shipped software. [ACH, 2025-01-22](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.12862v1); [TDFlow, 2026-01-22 revision](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23761v2).
- **B for accounting, D for savings:** Section 06's cache prices and TTL distinction are supported. Its 22.6% tick reduction and 6.4% expired-child bucket are modeled opportunities, not measured net savings. Removing a notification turn can remove useful work; replacing a child adds briefing, writes and rediscovery. Test liveness and total task cost before changing the rule. [Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing), [caching documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching), accessed 2026-11-10.
- **B for local counts:** Sections 06 and 07 do not implement a semantically shared schema. Input tokens, assistant messages, errors, corrections, model lists and result sizes differ. The headline totals reconcile with their own retained rows, but provider comparisons require normalization.
- **C for local review observations, A for external experiments:** Additional review can find something useful and can change correct code incorrectly. Local finding entries and scores do not establish review precision or marginal yield. The six Claude ledgers retain ten open findings, so they cannot demonstrate universal convergence or a causal benefit from the round cap. [Cross-model review, 2026-07-22](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21656v1).
- **A with task limits:** The literature does not say that all fan-out hurts correctness. Results depend on task divisibility, shared decisions and verification. The 180 versus 260 configuration counts in sections 05 and 01 describe different paper versions. Read-only reviewers still need their findings checked. [Original scaling paper, 2025-12-09](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08296v1); [revision, 2026-04-08](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08296v3).
- **C for source identification:** Lauren Tan's Cursor affiliation and pstack connection are confirmed. Theo's council really does request three providers, high effort, read-only reviews, scores, synthesis and repeated review until satisfaction. Neither establishes a measured advantage for the exact recipe. The user's intended Lauren clip and Theo's reported UI experiment durations remain unconfirmed. [Maven workshop, 2026-08-12](https://maven.com/p/e23d9c/how-cursor-turned-ai-agents-into-better-engineers); [council source, accessed 2026-11-10](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/t3dotgg/tstack/main/skills/council/SKILL.md).
- **A/B externally, no local outcome measure:** Section 08 overstates several transfers. Interview structure research evaluates employers' selection methods, not STAR preparation. The observation experiment used students and did not test weekly mocks. The Indeed percentages describe all occupations. These distinctions change the strength of the recommendations, even where the underlying numbers are real.
- **D for project benefit:** Evidence bundles, ordered stories and moldable views remain plausible experiments. Repeated reading supports investigating them; it does not validate their cost or architecture. Restore the actual diff-core and residual-cli drafts before committing to new interfaces.
